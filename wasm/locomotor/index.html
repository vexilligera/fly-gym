What we added, and what remains assumed

The circuit contains 16 descending neurons, 622 interneurons, 220 motor neurons, 153 sensory neurons and 34 ascending neurons. Synaptic transmission produces motor firing; antagonist motor pools produce joint forces; realized joint motion and contact return as sensory input.

The selected neurons and connections come from MaleCNS. Electrical dynamics, normalized synaptic gains, pooled sensory tuning, muscle forces and body mechanics are supplied model choices. Our four unresolved claw sensory cells remain unlabeled. This experiment tests motor control under artificial descending stimulation; it does not add autonomous sugar navigation or connect this subset to the full FlyWire brain.
