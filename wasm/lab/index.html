Where is the biological brain?

A real fly integrates vision, smell, and body feedback in its brain and ventral nerve cord (VNC). Descending neurons help select and steer behavior; local leg circuits coordinate movement.

This demo substitutes your commands for brain-level decisions and uses mathematical oscillators plus recorded step trajectories. It has no whole-brain connectome or spiking neurons. Vision, smell, and sensory reflex corrections are not active here.

MuJoCo does use joint error and contact in its mechanical calculation. FlyGym also includes a hybrid controller that uses sensory feedback to correct stumbling.
